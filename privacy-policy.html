Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Re Create Technologies handles information you provide when you visit our website, contact our team or enquire about our products and services.

Last updated: September 2026
Your privacy

We aim to handle information responsibly and use it only for legitimate business and communication purposes.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you contact Re Create Technologies, request information, ask for a quotation or communicate with our team.

Depending on how you interact with us, this information may include:

  • Your name and business or company name.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Information included in enquiries, messages or contact forms.
  • Details about the product or service you are interested in.
  • Other information you choose to provide to us.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information provided to Re Create Technologies may be used to respond to your enquiries and communicate with you about our products, services and business solutions.

We may use information to:

  • Respond to questions, enquiries and quotation requests.
  • Provide information about requested products or services.
  • Communicate with clients regarding projects or services.
  • Improve our website, services and customer experience.
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of our website.
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

3. Website Data and Cookies

Like many websites, our website may use cookies or similar technologies that support website functionality, security, preferences and performance.

Your browser may allow you to control or disable cookies. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features or functionality.

4. How We Share Information

Re Create Technologies does not sell personal information provided through this website.

Information may be shared with service providers or business partners where reasonably necessary to operate our website, communicate with clients or provide requested services.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process or a lawful request from an appropriate authority.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss or disclosure.

However, no internet transmission, electronic communication or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms or services, including communication and social media platforms.

When you leave our website or interact with a third-party service, that provider's own privacy practices and terms may apply. Re Create Technologies does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party websites.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information held about you.

You may contact us if you want to ask about information you previously provided, request a correction, or raise a privacy-related concern.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, maintaining business records and meeting applicable legal obligations.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and business services are intended for general business audiences and are not specifically directed toward children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or privacy practices change. The updated version may be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, you can contact Re Create Technologies.
